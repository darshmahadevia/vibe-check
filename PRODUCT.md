# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User specified Next.js App Router, TypeScript, and Vercel Hobby. No database or app accounts.

## Users

People choosing music for a photographed moment. Hackathon judges can use anonymous basic mode. An allowlisted Premium teammate tests queue mode.

## Product purpose

Vibe Check selects a song for a photo and exposes the evidence, six mood parameters, weights, candidate scores, and winner comparison.

## Capabilities and constraints

Photo uploads use browser JPEG conversion at a maximum of 768 pixels. Gemma 4 proposes eight songs in one normal call. Code ranks songs. Spotify search verifies candidates anonymously. Browser PKCE enables queueing. Photos are never stored or logged by the app. Rooms, voting, accounts, databases, playlists, history, video, native apps, and payments are excluded.

## Brand commitments

The name is Vibe Check. The user requests a polished music workbench with a pitch-black background, Spotify-inspired dark surfaces and green accents, white text, and readable neutral gray text. Photo and confirmed queued album covers stay fully visible through a persistent thumbnail selector. Mood comparisons use plainly labeled photo and song bars on the same scale. Purposeful motion respects reduced-motion preferences.
